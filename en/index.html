How it works

From a voice to four lines of captions

One transcript, many languages: every translation comes from the same sentence, so they agree with each other and extra languages barely add to the cost.

  1. 01

    Sound from the room and online

    A microphone in the room, the computer’s own audio or the sound of a single app such as Zoom. Captions tell the speaker in the room from the one joining remotely.

  2. 02

    A transcript that knows the subject

    The speech model writes as people talk and knows the event’s description, the speakers’ names and the terms in the module. At each pause it repairs misheard words and drops fillers.

  3. 03

    Up to four languages at once

    The same transcript becomes captions in up to four languages: in a transparent overlay above the slides, on a projector, an LED wall or in Zoom’s caption bar.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which languages can the captions be in?

In 85 languages, up to four at once on the same screen. Speakers can talk in any of them and switch while they speak; the interpreter detects the language itself.

How quickly do captions appear?

The spoken language is written while it is spoken. Translations follow within about a second and refresh during longer sentences; at the end of each sentence the interpreter polishes them.

Does it understand the terminology of my field?

Yes. Seven terminology modules are built in (accounting, medicine, law, technology, finance, industry and science), and several can be on at once. For every event we add the event description, the speakers’ names, and terms and acronyms with fixed translations if you want them.

What do we need at the event?

An audio source (a mixing desk or a microphone), a screen or projector for the captions and a stable internet connection. For a turnkey event we bring the computer and an operator.

Does it work for hybrid and online events?

Yes. Captions can sit in a transparent overlay above the slides, on a separate screen or in Zoom’s caption bar. Afterwards you get the transcript as SRT, WebVTT, Markdown or text.

Can the captions match our brand?

Yes. We adapt the size, layout, colours, backing and position of the captions to the room and your brand, and save them in an event profile.

What about the speakers’ privacy?

Audio goes only to the engine you choose, the app collects no telemetry, and session history is off by default and never contains audio. For the strictest requirements there is a local engine that keeps the audio on the computer.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the length of the event, the number of rooms and languages, and whether you want an operator. Send us an inquiry and we will prepare a tailored quote.
